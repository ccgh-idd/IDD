Understanding Dynamics

Understanding the underlying ecological and transmission dynamics is often key to the design of effective control strategies for infectious disease. The IDD lab at Charité Center for Global Health connects an interdisciplinary group of experts in mathematical modelling, statistics, clinical trials, and policy.

We work in close connection with other centres and international collaborators to increase our understanding of disease dynamics and thereby disease prevention. We also host the secretariat to Charite's IDD theme, the Berlin Infectious Disease Dynamics Expertise network (B-IDDE) and the Pneumococcal and Pneumonia INterest Group (PING). We are also an associated member of the Modeling Network for Severe Infectious Diseases (MONID).
